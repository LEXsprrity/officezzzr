# Steal a Seed

A Roblox "steal-and-run" game in Luau, built with [Rojo](https://rojo.space) 7. It replaces Coin Rush.

Players get a fenced plot. From spawn, one long walled corridor runs through seven themed zones. Each zone has a sleeping giant guardian in its nest against the left wall of the corridor, its seed pods in a loose little cluster right beside it, at its right. Grab a seed pod, the guardian wakes and chases you, and you run home and plant it. A seed only says its rarity ("Rare Seed"): the flower it grows into is drawn at random when you plant it, a surprise, and its name pops up over it. It sprouts into a plant-creature with a weight ("2,018,798 Kg") that earns money. Money buys **trails** (they multiply the Speed you gain by moving) and **plot upgrades** (more slots, more income); Speed gets you further down the corridor, and further zones grow better seeds. **The goal is to reach the Spirit Garden, the last stage, and grow its seeds.**

| # | Zone | Guardian | Seed | Recommended Speed | Length (studs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🌼 Meadow | Giant Turtle | Sproutling | 0 | 100 |
| 2 | 🦂 Desert | Red Mole | Cactopod | 25K | 200 |
| 3 | 🦖 Jungle | Giant Snake | Fernosaur | 500K | 400 |
| 4 | 🌋 Lava Fields | Volcano Gorilla | Magmabloom | 2M | 400 |
| 5 | ❄️ Snow Peaks | Ice Shark | Frostbulb | 7.5M | 400 |
| 6 | 👽 Deep Space | Void Dragon | Starpetal | 25M | 400 |
| 7 | ⛩️ Spirit Garden (END) | Lumistag | Lotus Wyrm | 75M | 400 |

Zone lengths come from a formula (`Config.zoneLength`): the first zone is the shortest (`ZONE_LENGTH_BASE` = 100 studs, so a new player grabs a first seed within seconds), each next zone is twice as long (`ZONE_LENGTH_GROWTH` = 2) up to `ZONE_LENGTH_MAX` = 400 (about two corridor widths deep), and every later zone stays at the max. The corridor is 2,300 studs long, so a run to the end takes about 13 s at the Spirit Garden's recommended Speed (WalkSpeed 171) and at max WalkSpeed (180). There are no zone gates, arches or name signs: one zone flows into the next by its floor and wall colours (a banner on the screen names the zone you enter), and a small studded orange plank (6 × 3.5, chest height) on a short post just in front of each sleeping boss (a few studs from its nose, a little to its left) says its recommended Speed ("👟 700K recommended"; the Meadow's says "Start here"), green once your Speed reaches it and red below. Streaming is off and the air is thin, so from spawn you can see down the whole corridor to the Spirit Garden's back wall.

## Seed packs
Our version of "eggs with odds": **a premium, late-game pack, Robux only**. Each pack is a developer product, and there is no way to buy one with in-game money. The 🛒 Shop starts with the **-- FEATURED --** section (the Shop's Featured tab scrolls there), which shows the Starpetal pack as a big card in its zone colours, with its exact odds table and two buttons: **1 Seed** and **10 Seeds** (owner 2026-10-15, like the reference's "1 Egg" / "10 Eggs"), each a gift + R$ button.

**The Lotus Wyrm pack is retired** (owner 2026-10-15: new players shouldn't skip the game by buying the last zone's seeds). `PackSpirit` / `PackSpirit10` stay in `Config.DEVELOPER_PRODUCTS` with `retired = true`: no Shop card or button (`Config.forSale` / `SeedPacks.offers` leave them out), `Shop.canPrompt` refuses them ("isn't sold any more"), but a receipt for one (a purchase already in flight, a pending receipt) still grants and is never lost. On Roblox they are taken off sale: `monetization/catalog.json` has `"isForSale": false` for them and `node tools/create_monetization.mjs --universe <id> --update-prices` PATCHes `isForSale=false`. To sell it again: remove `retired` from both, `lune run tools/export_monetization.luau`, run `--update-prices` again (it puts them back on sale).

| Pack | Products | Rolls | Price (x1 / x10) | Rarity odds | Mutations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 👽 Starpetal Pack | `PackSpace`, `PackSpace10` | 1 or 10 Deep Space (zone 6) seeds | 200 / 1,500 R$ | Common 20%, Uncommon 23.3%, Rare 26.1%, Epic 29.2%, **Legendary 1.5%** | 🌟 Celestial 0.625%, Gold 10%, Diamond 4%, Rainbow 2.5% |
| ⛩️ Lotus Wyrm Pack (**retired**, not sold) | `PackSpirit`, `PackSpirit10` | 1 or 10 Spirit Garden (zone 7) seeds | 300 / 2,299 R$ | Common 18.2%, Uncommon 22.4%, Rare 26.5%, Epic 31.4%, **Legendary 1.5%** | 🌟 Celestial 0.625%, Gold 10%, Diamond 4%, Rainbow 2.5% |

- **No Speed gate.** Anyone can buy the Starpetal pack, even at 0 Speed: a premium shortcut to late-game seeds (the last zone's are not sold).
- **Odds.** A pack rolls Common..Epic like a *night* pod in its zone (`PACK_LUCK = NIGHT_LUCK = 2`), but **Legendary is a fixed 1.5%** (`PACK_LEGENDARY_CHANCE`, about 1 in 67: better than any steal, still a rare event), with doubled Gold/Diamond/Rainbow chances (`PACK_MUTATION_MULT = 2`). The Night mutation never rolls from packs. The odds in the UI come from the same function the server rolls with (`SeedPacks.odds`).
- **🌟 Celestial** (×12 income), the rarest: both packs roll it at `PACK_EXCLUSIVE_CHANCE` (0.625%), and since 2026-10-19 pods roll it too, at half the Rainbow's chance (0.625%; owner: "let celestials appear on stolen as well"). Packs don't double it (it is `exclusive`).
- **Prices.** The real price is whatever you set on the Creator Dashboard. The Shop fetches it once per product with `MarketplaceService:GetProductInfo` (pcall'd and cached) and shows it as `R$ 1,500`. Until that works, or while the id is still `0`, it shows the placeholder `robux` from `Config.DEVELOPER_PRODUCTS`.
- **Buying.** Clicking a pack with `id = 0` shows a "coming soon" toast. With a real id, the server checks `Shop.canPrompt` (a known product, and all its seeds fit under `SEED_BAG_MAX`: a x10 needs 10 free spaces, or it says "A x10 pack needs 10 free spaces in your seed bag (you have N)") and calls `PromptProductPurchase`. `ProcessReceipt` grants. A receipt that arrives is **always** granted, because the player paid.
- **Receipts.** `ProcessReceipt` grants once per `PurchaseId` (the last 100 ids are kept in the profile), saves, and only then returns `PurchaseGranted`. If the save fails it returns `NotProcessedYet`, and a retry in the same session doesn't grant twice. If the server dies before saving, neither the seed nor the id were saved, so the retry on the next server grants exactly once.
- **Where the seed goes.** Into a persisted **seed bag** (`profile.seedBag`, stacks by `zone:rarity[:Mutation]`). Press **Plant** on it in the Plants panel's **Inventory** tab, then click or tap your plot's dirt: the same rules as a carried seed (see "Planting"). `SEED_BAG_MAX` (50) is a soft cap: the Shop won't *prompt* past it, but paid grants go past it up to `SEED_BAG_HARD_MAX` (250). A pack whose seed doesn't fit at the hard max pays money instead: `PACK_FULL_BAG_INCOME_SECONDS` (300 s) of your current income, or 300 s of a Common plant from that pack's zone if that's more (so a player with no plants still gets something). A paid pack is never lost, and a toast says what happened.
- **x10.** One receipt rolls all 10 seeds (`count = 10`, `Shop` grants them in one go, once per `PurchaseId`), each into the bag; a seed that can't fit at the hard max is paid in money like above, so a x10 never takes Robux without granting all of it. Every Legendary among them is announced.
- **Reveal.** A reel spins through candidate seeds, lands on the server's result and bursts in its rarity colour. A x10 spins fast to its best seed, then shows all 10 in a 2x5 grid (their 3D seed looks on rarity-coloured cards, the best one in a gold frame with a BEST! tag) and "Click to continue".

## Live events: "admin abuse" in every server
Owner 2026-10-19: "ADMIN PANEL WITH ADMIN ABUSE ... LIVE EVENTS WITH LIVE NOTIS ACROSS SERVERS". The admin panel's **EVENTS** tab (F2, then EVENTS) starts a timed event (1–30 minutes) in **every server at once**; everyone gets a big "ADMIN ABUSE! 3x LUCK for 10 minutes!" banner with a chime, a strip of the running events with a live countdown sits under the DayTimer (top right; under the Tutorial banner while that shows), and "Event ended" shows when one ends or is stopped. Everything is in `Shared/LiveEvents` (pure: checks, merge, multipliers), `Server/LiveEventService` (sync) and `Client/LiveEvents` (banner, strip).

| Event | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Luck | 2x, 3x, 5x | Stolen seeds re-draw their rarity at the pod's luck x the 2x Luck pass x the event (`Config.luckUpgrade`, never lower than the pod showed; the Legendary chance scales too) |
| Mutation | 2x, 3x, 5x, or All Gold / Diamond / Rainbow / Celestial | xN: a plain stolen seed may mutate so each mutation is N times as likely overall (Haunted never; at most 90% in all); All X: every stolen seed is X. A seed that goes back to its pod goes back as the pod showed it |
| Money | 2x, 3x, 5x | Plant income in the payout loop x N (on top of plot level and passes). Offline pay, sell prices and saves are untouched |
| Speed | 2x, 3x, 5x | Speed from walking and the treadmill x N |
| Grow | 2x, 3x, 5x, Instant | Seeds planted during it sprout in time / N (on top of Fast Grow), or are flowers at once |
| Candy | Rain (only while Halloween runs) | 40 candy at once, then one every 3 s, up to 60 more on the map |
| Message | the SEND box | A message (max 120 characters, filtered with `TextService:FilterStringAsync` for broadcast) shown big in every server |

Two events of one kind give the bigger multiplier, never the product. Every multiplier is clamped to 2x–5x by the server, minutes to 1–30, and only admins (AdminService's list, re-checked by LiveEventService, rate-limited) can start, stop or announce. Nothing is saved into a profile.

**Sync.** Each event has an id and absolute `startedAt` / `endsAt` (server time, `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()`), so every server ends it at the same moment. The server that starts or stops one applies it at once, writes it into the MemoryStore HashMap `LiveEvents_v1` (key `active`, every unexpired event; a stopped one stays there as a tombstone until its end) and publishes it on the MessagingService topic `LiveEvents`. Every server merges what it hears by id (a stop wins), reads the store when it starts (events still running) and every 30 s (a lost message). Every call is pcall'd with retries; in Studio, where MessagingService doesn't work, the event still runs in that server. Clients read `ReplicatedStorage.LiveEvents` (a Configuration per running event).

## Planting: anywhere on your dirt
Each plot (owner's reference) is fenced with a chunky studded wooden fence and has **two brown dirt beds**, left and right, with a grass path down the middle from the entrance. You plant **anywhere on your own beds**, not in fixed slots:
- **Preview.** After picking a seed (hotbar slot **2**, or **Plant** on a seed or plant in the Inventory; or while carrying one), `Planting.client` shows a translucent **3D ghost** of exactly the plant that will grow, facing the way plants face, where the mouse points on your dirt (on a phone: where you last tapped; on a gamepad: just in front of you), **ghost-green** if it fits and **red** if not. Every seed has a hidden **gene** from the moment it exists (a pod's roll, a pack; `Config.rollGene`, kept in the carry and in the bag key `zone:rarity[:Mutation]#gene`, save v10): it decides its flower, its weight variance and its seed design, so the ghost is that flower at the size it has right when planted (an inventory plant: its own flower and size). The name stays a surprise: every label still says "Rare Seed" until it is planted, when the flower's name pops up for 2 s. The server builds a ghost per flower the first time a seed needs it (`Server/PlantGhosts.ensure` into `ReplicatedStorage.PlantGhosts`, plain inert parts). A bag seed from before v10 gets its gene when you pick it. A refused click shows why in small relief text near the bottom; there is no hint box. Click / tap / **R2** plants. **Q** or **Cancel** cancels a picked seed.
- **Rules** (`Shared/Planting.check`, the same on the client and the server): the spot is on one of **your own** plot's beds (`Role = "PlantBed"`), with `Planting.bedMargin` of dirt around it (`PLANT_BED_MARGIN`, 1 stud, for every rarity: a Legendary may stand by the fence, its leaves reaching over it; owner 2026-10-16); at least `Planting.minDistance` from every other plant: a tiny core, `max(PLANT_MIN_GAP, PLANT_SPACING_SHARE x (reachA + reachB))` = max(0.8, 0.06 x the two full-grown reaches), so plants may touch and their leaves overlap freely (two Commons 0.8 studs apart, two Legendaries ~1.2; owner 2026-10-16: "i want to be able to grow them close") and only a spot right over a neighbour's centre is refused; within `PLANT_REACH` (28 studs, flat) of where you stand; and the plot has a free slot (`Sessions.maxSlots`: plot level + ExtraSlots pass). The server re-checks all of it on every `PlantSeed` (rate-limited, the position must be a real finite Vector3) and toasts the reason when it refuses; a refused seed stays carried or in the bag.
- **Saved spots.** A plant saves where it stands in the plot's own space (origin: the floor's top centre, looking in from the entrance), so it lands in the same place on whatever plot index you get next time. It keeps an id (`slot`, the smallest free number) that the Plants panel's Remove button, the plants view and the SELL stall use. Old saves: see "Old saves".
- **Inventory** (`Server/Inventory`, save v9). The Plants panel has two tabs, **Plants** and **Inventory** (the old Seeds side menu is gone). **Remove** on a plant takes it off the plot into the inventory as it is (its flower, mutation and growth: its age stops, its weight variance is kept), for **no money**; at most `INVENTORY_MAX` (50), and Remove is off while it's full. The Inventory lists the seed bag's seeds ("Rare Seed x2 · Desert") and the removed plants (by flower), each with a **Plant** button that starts the ghost preview; a plant planted again keeps its flower, mutation and weight and grows on. The **SELL stall** lists planted and inventory plants and pays the same price for either.
- **Home with a seed.** A stolen seed goes into the seed bag by itself the moment you cross from the corridor into the base (`SeedService.storeCarried`, checked 5 times a second), free slots or not, so it never stays on your back; its pod regrows. Plant it from hotbar slot **2** or the Inventory. With the bag at `SEED_BAG_MAX` it stays carried, and the Inventory and a relief line near the bottom say so.
- **Seeds look.** Pods and the carried seed are biome seeds (`assets/Seed_<ZoneId>`, 2 designs per zone, none shared between zones; `tools/export_seeds.luau`): our own part-built designs (`tools/seed_designs.luau`: clover, daisy, cactus, pitcher plant, obsidian, frost pine, ice crystal, planet, alien, sakura, lotus) and the three bought ones the owner kept (Sunpetal, Dawnfruit, Amberpine; preview: `tools/seed_sheet.luau`), the gene picking the design whatever the rarity (the rarity shows only in the name tag). No Neon, no light. Pod name tags are relief text, always drawn on top, at staggered heights.


## Sprouting and "Grow now" (save v12)
A seed you plant (carried home, or from the bag) first stands as a **seedling**: a little stepped mound of studded dirt with a sprout, two leaves and a bud in its rarity's colour (its mutation's if it has one; `Server/Seedling`). A seedling **earns nothing**. When its time is up it pops into its flower (the owner hears it and sees "Grown!" rise) and only then starts earning, its growth curve (`GROW_TIME`) starting from that moment. Hover (or tap) a seedling: over its name a relief countdown ("2:31") shows, and on **your own** seedlings a green **Grow now** button with its Robux price (`Client/Sprouts`, a PlayerGui billboard because world billboards take no clicks; it lingers 0.7 s so the mouse can reach it). The Plants panel's card of a seedling says "Sprouting 2:31" instead of its $/s and has the same button.
- **Time** (`Config.sproutTime(zone, rarity)`): by rarity only, the same in every zone (owner 2026-10-19: long sprouts were driving kids away, at most 5 minutes), rounded to 5 s. The Fast Grow pass halves it. It is an absolute time (`sproutUntil`), so it goes on **offline**; offline pay counts each plant only for the part of the time away after it sprouted (`PlotService.offlineEarnings`). Seedlings saved with more than 5 min left are cut to it.

| Common | Uncommon | Rare | Epic | Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 s | 15 s | 30 s | 1:00 | 5:00 |

- **Balance.** The free-player simulation (`tests/progression_sim`) plants every seed as a seedling: with the short sprouts the Spirit Garden comes at about **12h 18m** (every zone inside its ±50% window), so nothing else was retuned.
- **Inventory.** Remove on a seedling keeps what it had left (`sprout`, seconds); it waits in the inventory (the card says "Sprouts in 2:31") and sprouts on for that long when planted again.
- **Grow now** (developer products, kind `GrowNow`): `GrowNow10` "Skip (1)" **9 R$** while 1 minute or less is left (Common to Epic), `GrowNow30` "Skip (2)" **19 R$** for anything longer, which finishes the seedling **however long it has left** (the Legendaries; and a "Grow now" that leaves a seedling still sprouting would read as broken to a kid). The button asks the server (`RequestBuy "GrowNow:<plant id>"`, rate-limited); `PlotService.promptGrowNow` checks it is **your own** plant and still sprouting and picks the product by its time left, and `Shop.canPrompt` re-checks the tier. The plant is remembered per product in the session; `ProcessReceipt` (idempotent per PurchaseId, saved before confirming) sprouts it at once. If it already sprouted or was sold or removed meanwhile (or the receipt lands after a rejoin), the purchase goes to the seedling with the most time left, on the plot first, then in the inventory, skipping up to what the product covers (1 min / all); with no seedling anywhere it is confirmed and logged (`[ShopService] Grow Now … no seedling left`): a game can't refund Robux.
- **Old saves.** Plants from before v12 have no `sproutUntil`: they are fully grown, as they were.

## First-time tutorial
Owner 2026-10-19: "u just really need 5 steps of the tutorial, go get first seed, go plant first seed, go walk on treadmill, done", with "the 3d arrows pointing to the seeds are enough". `Client/Tutorial` shows it while the profile's `tutorialDone` is false. Each step has a trail of glowing red chevrons on the ground from the player to its target, flowing toward it (no words over the target), and one studded banner at the very top of the screen with the instruction and a **Skip** button. The trail goes out of the plot by its entrance and through the corridor mouth (`Tutorial.route` / `Tutorial.arrows`). Between steps the trail and the banner fade out and back in. A step advances when the player actually does the thing (`Tutorial.advance`):

| # | Step | Banner (target) | Advances when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | steal | "Follow the red arrows to steal a seed!" (a Meadow pod) | StatsUpdate `carrying` (they hold a seed) |
| 2 | home | "Run home with your seed..." (their plot) | LootEvent `Stored` (the seed went into the bag); a seed lost on the way (caught, dropped, died) goes back to step 1 |
| 3 | plant | "Press 2, then click your brown dirt..." (their dirt, plus a red arrow over the hotbar's seed slot) | LootEvent `Planted` |
| 4 | speed | "Walk on your treadmill to get faster!" (their treadmill) | +300 Speed since the step began (walking or the treadmill) |
| 5 | ready | "All done! Get 25K Speed to reach the Desert." (the Desert's Speed sign) | the **Let's go!** button, or reaching the sign |

Finishing or skipping fires the `Tutorial` remote. `Server/TutorialService` then sets `tutorialDone`, and the next save keeps it. **Old saves** have no `tutorialDone` field: they count as done when the player clearly played (any money, a plant, an inventory plant, a seed in the bag, an Index entry, or 1,000+ Speed; `Tutorial.doneFromSave`), so veterans never see it. A player who leaves half way picks up from their stats (`Tutorial.startStep`).

## Economy: what money and Robux buy
**In-game money buys only three things: trails, plot upgrades and treadmill tiers.** Everything else in the Shop is Robux (developer products and gamepasses). There is no money path to Speed or extra slots (`Shop.buy` refuses them, and tests check it).

### 🏃 Trails (money or Robux)
A trail is a coloured `Trail` that streams behind your character (two Attachments on the HumanoidRootPart, made by the server so everyone sees it, rebuilt on every respawn and every equip). The equipped trail multiplies the Speed you **gain** from walking and the treadmill: a small multiplier on the flat Speed per step (x1 to x3, see Progression). You own trails (saved) and equip one at a time. Any tier can be bought directly, without the one below it. Buying one equips it.

Toasts strip any emoji the server sends (except the lock). `tests/hud.spec.luau` scans the client scripts for emoji outside those places.

**Bat, collisions and knockback** (owner 2026-10-16): the Bat is a real Tool (`BatTool`, model `assets/Bat.rbxm`): 1 equips it, a click swings it (`BatSwing.client`, a strike on the shoulder joint, passed on to every client). Players walk through each other (collision group Players x Players off). The big set pieces (trees, rocks, volcanoes, pyramids, crystals, shrines: `SolidProps.SLOTS`) turn solid when the server starts, in the "Decor" group that guards pass through. The zone banner stays.

Only what the player must be told is toasted: the server's messages (mostly why something was refused), getting caught, offline earnings, day/night and server-wide announcements. Stealing, planting, selling, removing, buying, the bat and guards waking are seen in the world and heard (Effects.client): no toast, and no banner while you carry a seed. Walking back into the base shows no banner either.

**Plants are named after their flower.** Each plant grows into one of its zone × rarity's flower models (`assets/Creature_<Seed>.rbxm`, picked by its id), and that model's name is what the game calls it: the billboard over it (shown while the mouse is over the plant, or for 3 s after a tap), the Plants panel, the Sell cards ("Daisy", "Gold Desert Wild Flower"; `PlotService.plantName`, `CreatureModels.variantOf`, `Format.plantName`). ### 🏃 Treadmill upgrades (money or Robux)
Standing on **your own** plot's treadmill pays a **fixed Speed every step for its level**, times your equipped trail (x1 Grey … x3 Rainbow): a step is one server tick (`Config.TICK` = 0.25 s, so 4 steps a second), AFK included. Owner decision (2026-10-15): **the number shown is the number you get**: the `+X` over the belt, on its board, on the Shop card and on every popup over the runner is the same `+X`, and every tick pays exactly it (`Config.treadmillPerStep`; a second pays 4× it, `Config.treadmillPerSecond`). Nothing else changes it: not your WalkSpeed, not the Speed you already have. Only your own treadmill pays: someone else's gives nothing, doesn't play the run-in-place animation, and you can't step onto it. There are 10 levels (`Config.TREADMILL_TIERS`), bought one at a time:

An AFK farmer, who fills the plot once and then only stands on the treadmill buying the same upgrades, is behind the active player at every zone from the Jungle on and takes about 2.4x as long to reach the Spirit Garden (30 h vs 12h 41m; it was ~5x before steps paid in full: AFK is much stronger now, owner-accepted): the corridor's better plants are what pay for the better treadmill.

### Gamepasses
Six passes (`Config.GAMEPASSES`, ids `0` until created; prices in the setup checklist below). The Shop's **-- PASSES --** section has one bright card per pass in its own colour (`Shared/PassCards`): icon (VIP: a gold relief "VIP" badge), name, one benefit line and a green R$ button with the price (`GetProductInfo(id, GamePass)`, cached; the placeholder until then), or a grey **Owned**. Ownership is decided only on the server: `UserOwnsGamePassAsync` at join (`ShopService.checkPasses`) and again when `PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished` reports a purchase (that report comes from the buyer's client, so on its own it grants nothing; a pass with id `0` never matches), into `Sessions.passes`; `Pass:<name>` from the client only opens the prompt (or says "coming soon" while the id is `0`; never for a pass you own). Everything multiplies (`Config.passSpeedMult` / `passMoneyMult`): 2x Speed + VIP = x2.5 Speed, 2x Money + VIP = x2.5 money, on top of the trail and the plot level.
- **2x Speed** (`DoubleSpeed`): every Speed gain x2, walking (`Config.speedPerStud`) and treadmill (`Config.treadmillPerStep`), applied in `SpeedService` through `Sessions.speedMult` / `speedPerStud`. The belt's `+X`, the runner's popups, the treadmill board, the Shop's treadmill card and `+X/step` all show the owner's doubled number.
- **2x Luck** (`DoubleLuck`): pods are shared, so the luck applies when the owner **steals**. The pod keeps the seed it showed everyone; for the owner its rarity is re-drawn by `Config.luckUpgrade`, a quantile coupling: the pod's rarity is a slice of the world-luck odds, a point drawn in that slice is read on the double-luck odds. It can only move up, and over many steals the owner's rarities follow the double-luck odds exactly (Common..Epic tilted with luck x2, Legendary chance x2), with no extra re-roll on top. The draw is fixed by the seed's gene and the thief (`SeedService.luckRoll`), and a seed that goes back to its pod (caught) goes back as the pod showed it, so getting caught and stealing again never re-rolls it.
- **VIP**: a gold relief **VIP** tag over the head (`Server/VipService`, put back on every respawn), a gold **[VIP]** chat tag (`VipChat.client`, `TextChatService.OnIncomingMessage`, from the player's server-set `VIP` attribute), x1.25 money and x1.25 Speed.
- **Fast Grow** (`FastGrow`): the owner's plants age 2x as fast (`Config.plantAge`, read from `plantedAt`), so they are full-grown in 10 minutes, and the time offline counts the same (offline pay uses the plants as they grew). Remove keeps the growth they had; planted again they go on at 2x. Their seeds sprout in half the time too (`Config.sproutTime`).
- **2x Money** and **+10 Plant Slots**: as before.

### 💰 Selling plants: the SELL stall
The shop plaza's 4th stall (like `docs/references/09-sell-stall-reference.png`) is an open wooden stall with a red-and-white striped awning, a soft green glowing circle on the floor in front of it and a big red outlined **SELL** floating above. Its prompt (`OpensShop = "Sell"`, **E**) opens the **Sell** window: one card per plant (planted or in the Inventory), then a **Seeds** group with one card per kind of seed in your bag ("Rare Seed" / "x3 · Desert", its seed design), cheapest first. Every card's body is its **rarity's colour** (studded, a shade darker than its title band: Common grey, Uncommon green, Rare blue, Epic purple, Legendary gold); a mutation shows as its coloured border and word. A plant card: $/s (muted grey "Night x0.8" / "Day x0.8" out of its phase, "In your Inventory", "Sprouting"), the price and a red **Sell**; a seed card: the price of one, **Sell 1** and **Sell all**. A second click ("Sure?") sells. Above them the **quick sells**: **Sell Common / Uncommon / Rare / Epic** and **Sell all** (every rarity but Legendary, which is never quick-sold), each saying how many things it sells and for how much, or greyed with "nothing to sell"; they take only what is in the bag, inventory plants and seeds, never a planted plant (owner 2026-10-20: those sell one by one from their card), and keep mutated ones unless **Include mutated** is ON. They ask first in a confirm bar inside the window (no system dialogs). What each button sells is `Shared/SellPick` (tested end to end through the remote). The window scrolls, scales down on phones, and updates the prices in place on each payout instead of rebuilding.

- **Price** (owner 2026-10-15: 2 minutes paid far too much). A plant sells for **10× its $/s**: `Config.SELL_STALL_SECONDS` = **10 s** of its current base income (it grows with weight; mutation included; no day/night) × the plot level and money passes (`Selling.price`). A seedling sells as the fresh plant it will be. A **seed** sells for `SEED_SELL_SECONDS` = **5 s** of the income it would have right as it sprouts (sprout weight, mutation included) × the same multiplier, at least $1 (`Config.seedSellPrice`): planted, even with the lowest weight variance, it would sell for more, so a seed is always worth less than its plant.
- **Server checks** (`SellPlants(slots, ids?, seeds?)`, rate-limited like the other remotes): plain lists of at most 40 plant slots and 50 inventory ids (whole numbers; only yours, each sold once even if named twice) and `seeds` = `{ ["zone:rarity[:Mutation]"]: count }` (gene-less labels, whole counts 1–250; a count above what you hold sells what you hold, never more; anything malformed refuses the whole request). A repeated request for something already sold pays nothing (tests/security.spec drives the real handler 10 times in a row). The Sell window hides what it has just asked to sell (a seed card counts down) until the server's next push confirms it, so spamming Sell can't look like selling the same thing again; a repeat click on it sends nothing, and the quick sells count without it (`SellPick.unsold`; shown again after 6 s if the server refused). You must stand within `SELL_STALL_DISTANCE` (20 studs) of a SELL stall's prompt part (`WorldBuilder.sellPoints`). The check and the change happen together, with no yield in between (`Selling.sell`). Selling frees the slots, removes the creatures, refreshes income, the plants view and the bag (saved by the autosave), and the client gets the cash sound and a +$ popup over the stall.
- The stall is the `ShopStall_Sell` asset slot, with no `ShopStall` fallback: it keeps its own look until it gets its own model. The circle and the SELL text stay from code.

### 🎁 Community rewards: Free Gift, MOST MONEY/s
Like `docs/references/06-free-gift-like-favorite-join.png` and `06-free-chest-and-leaderboard.png`. Rules in `Rewards` (pure, tested), Roblox side in `RewardService`, UI in `Rewards.client` (its own script: `HUD.client` is near Luau's 200-locals limit; same window style, drawn inside the HUD's ScreenGui so it shares its scale and the toasts show over it).

**Free Gift window.** The pink **gift box** opens it. It stands where the free chest used to (owner request: no free chest, only the gift), against the inside of the front wall, left of the corridor mouth, by the spawn: `GiftBox` (server) builds it at runtime on the map's gift spot (an invisible part with Role `GiftSpot`, so moving that part in the map moves the gift), with a pink glowing circle, "FREE GIFT" floating above it and an **E** prompt ("Open", "View" once claimed; attribute `FreeGift`) that `Rewards.client` answers by opening the window. It also opens by itself once per account, `FREE_GIFT_AUTO_OPEN` = 20 s after joining. Cyan title bar "Free Gift!" with the red X, a big Speed bag, "~ LIMITED TIME! ~" in red and "25,000 SPEED!" in cyan-to-green, three step panels and a big **CLAIM!** button (grey **CLAIMED!** once done).
1. **Like the game!** Text only ("Tap 👍 on the game page"): a like can't be prompted or checked in game.
2. **Favorite the game!** A ⭐ Favorite button opens Roblox's own favourite prompt, `AvatarEditorService:PromptSetFavorite(game.PlaceId, Enum.AvatarItemType.Asset, true)` (the place is an asset; pcall'd; if it can't open, e.g. in Studio, the window says where the button is).
3. **Join the community!** A Join Community button. A group join can't be prompted in game, so it says how: game page → Community → Join `Config.COMMUNITY_NAME`.

**What the reward depends on: only the community (group).** CLAIM! sends `ClaimReward("Gift")`. The server checks `Player:IsInGroupAsync(Config.COMMUNITY_GROUP_ID)` (pcall'd; the deprecated `IsInGroup` as a fallback). Roblox can answer that from a per-session cache, so a "no" is re-checked with `GroupService:GetGroupsAsync`, which sees a group joined a minute ago. A "yes" is cached for the session and a "no" for 10 s. It grants `FREE_GIFT_SPEED` = **25,000 Speed** (the Desert's recommended Speed) once per account (the saved `freeGift` flag) and toasts. With `COMMUNITY_GROUP_ID = 0` (the repo default) the button says **COMING SOON** and the window doesn't open by itself.

Why the like and favourite are only asked for:
- **Likes can't be verified.** There is no API for them, so a "reward for liking" would pay everyone who clicks.
- **Paying for an action can break Roblox's rules.** Roblox's Community Rules don't allow prizes or giveaways "in exchange for something". Favourites can't be checked safely server-side either: a client-side check can be faked.
- **Community rewards are the usual, accepted case.** Rewarding a group join is the common way games promote their community, and it is checked on the server.

So a player who never likes or favourites still gets the gift by joining the community, and nobody gets it for a like or favourite alone. This follows the Community Rules and developer-forum consensus, not an official Roblox ruling: if Roblox's policy changes, `Rewards.claimGift` is the one place to change.

**No free chest.** The free chest (and its claim, countdown, sound and asset slot) is gone. Saves keep their `chestAt` field (v4) so old and new saves round-trip, but nothing reads it.

**MOST MONEY/s board.** A tall studded cyan board (frame slot `MoneyBoard`) right of the gift box. Green "MOST MONEY/s" title, then the **top 10 of all servers**, each row with rank, name, a green "Money/s" tag and the value ("$54.8T").
- **Writing:** every 60 s each server writes its players' current $/s to the OrderedDataStore `MostMoneyPerSecond_v1`. A player who never earned isn't written, and one who drops to $0 is written once. Values are stored as log10(1 + $/s) in fixed point, because OrderedDataStore only takes whole numbers and income goes past 2^63. The order is kept and it reads back to about 6 digits.
- **Reading:** each server then reads the top 10 back (`GetSortedAsync`), with every call pcall'd. Players in the server show their live value.
- **Status line:** "All servers · updates every minute".
- **Fallbacks:** until the first update the board says "Loading...". If the DataStore can't be used (Studio without API access, an outage), it shows the last list it read. If there is none, it shows this server's players with "This server · updates every minute".

This is the base's only leaderboard: the old "⚡ FASTEST HERE" board (Speed, this server) was removed.

### Old saves (format v13)
v13 keeps each plant's colour variant (`pa`, a `Shared/Palettes` id, in the inventory too). Older plants have none and stay Natural, the colours they always had; an unknown id loads as Natural.
v12 added sprouting: a plant saves `su` (`sproutUntil`, when its seedling becomes the flower; its `plantedAt` is the same time, where growth starts) and an inventory plant `sp` (seconds of sprouting it still had). Older plants have neither and are fully grown, as they were.
v10 gives every seed a hidden gene (its flower, size and seed design) and keeps it in the seed bag's key (`zone:rarity[:Mutation]#gene`). v9 bag keys load unchanged; such a seed gets its gene when you pick it to plant (or draws its flower at planting, as before).
v9 added the plant **inventory** (`inv`) and a plant's own weight `variance` (kept when it goes to the inventory and is planted again); v8 saves load with an empty inventory, and their plants keep the variance from their planting time and id (`Config.varianceOf`). v8 saves each plant's flower (`variant`, drawn when it is planted); older plants keep the flower their id picks.
v7 made the Speed per step flat and removed the Speed levels, which shrank the Speed scale (the recommended Speeds went from 10K … 5B to 25K … 75M). A v1–v6 save's Speed is moved onto the new scale once, on load (`ProfileStore.migrateSpeed`): between two zones' old recommended Speeds (`ProfileStore.LEGACY_ZONE_REQ`) it lands the same share of the way (on a log scale) between their new ones, so everyone keeps every zone they had reached and nobody overtakes anybody. Below the Desert it scales linearly; past the Spirit Garden it keeps the last stretch's slope. Examples: 3K → 7.5K, 10K → 25K, 100K → ~146K, 1M → ~758K, 10M → ~3M, 50M → 7.5M, 100M → ~10.8M, 1B → ~35M, 5B → 75M, 50B → ~225M. Money, trails (same ids; their multipliers are now x1–x3), plot level and treadmill tier are kept as they are.
v6 replaced the fixed plant slots with free planting: each plant saves where it stands (`px`, `pz` in the plot's own space) and keeps its old slot number as its id (so its weight doesn't change). A v1–v5 plant is put on its old slot's spot moved onto the new dirt beds (`Planting.legacySpot`: the 8-column grid's left half on the left bed, its right half on the right bed, rows unchanged), so an old plot keeps its arrangement. When a player joins, `Planting.settle` moves any plant that is off a bed or too close to another (a bed moved in Studio, a corrupt spot) to a free spot.
v5 grew the treadmill from 3 tiers to 10: a v1–v4 save's treadmill becomes the v5 tier with the same multiplier (`ProfileStore.migrateTreadmillTier`, `ProfileStore.TIER_MULT_V5`): the free x2 → tier 1, the Robux x3 → tier 5 (x3), the Robux x5 → tier 8 (x5). The tiers later became fixed Speed-per-second levels; a save keeps its `treadmillTier` number as it is (nothing to migrate). v4 added the community rewards: v3 saves load with the Free Gift unclaimed, its window not shown yet and the free chest ready.
Saves from before the rework (v1/v2) are migrated on load, once (the next save is the current format):
- **Speed Gain tier → trail**, the nearest multiplier at or below it (the trails' multipliers of the time, `ProfileStore.LEGACY_TRAIL_MULT`), owned and equipped: x1 → Grey (x1.5), x2 → Green (x2), x3 → Purple (x3), x5 → Rainbow (x5). Nobody's multiplier goes down, except that x10 and x25 are above the best trail: those players get Rainbow (x5) **and the money they paid for the x10/x25 tiers back** ($250M, and $250M + $10B).
- **Slots → plot level** = slots − 5 + 1 (clamped to 1–26), so everyone keeps exactly their slots. The plot's income multiplier comes with the level.
- Plants, Index, seed bag and receipts are kept as they are (the treadmill tier is mapped as above). Removed items (potions, Sleep Dust) are dropped from the inventory.

### Progression: a flat Speed per step (v7)
Money can't buy Speed, so Speed comes from moving, your treadmill (a fixed Speed every step, 4 steps a second, per level: +100 up to +300 with money or Robux levels) and Robux Speed packs. Every step pays the same **flat** amount, times your trail's small multiplier; nothing grows with the Speed you already have, and there are no Speed levels:

```
Speed per step = BASE_SPEED_PER_STUD (1) × trail (x1 Grey … x3 Rainbow)      (Config.speedPerStud)
walking        = studs walked × Speed per step
treadmill      = level's fixed step (+100 … +300) × trail, every tick (4/s)   (SpeedLogic.treadmillGain)
WalkSpeed      = clamp(16 + 27 × log10(1 + Speed / 100), 16, 180)          (Config.walkSpeedFor)
```

What still grows as you play: your WalkSpeed (more studs per second, so more steps while running: 16 at the start, ~81 at the Desert, ~175 at the Spirit Garden, 180 from ~120M), your trail (x1 → x3) and your treadmill level (+100 → +300 a step). The treadmill doesn't grow with WalkSpeed or Speed: only buying a level or a better trail raises it. The zones' recommended Speeds are on a much smaller scale than before v7 (v6 had a gain that compounded with Speed and reqs from 10K to 5B). The WalkSpeed curve was refitted so each zone's recommended Speed still runs within 10% of its old WalkSpeed (tested), which the guards are tuned for. Players see the gain: the Trail Shop shows **"+X/step"** and the treadmill its **"+X"** (`Treadmill.perStep`, the same number the server pays every tick and every popup shows).

**Tuning: a simulated free player** (`tests/progression_sim.luau`, asserted in `tests/progression.spec.luau`; `print(Sim.report())` prints these tables). The player is active and spends no Robux. ### The map: hand-editable, adopted at server start
`map/World.rbxm` is the **source of truth** for the map. Rojo places it at `Workspace.World`, so the whole map shows in Studio edit mode, and at server start `WorldBuilder.load()` **adopts** it as it is instead of rebuilding it. Edits made in Studio and saved back to the file (right-click **World** → Save to File → overwrite `map/World.rbxm`) stay forever: moved, rotated, deleted or added pieces, other people's models.

- **Gameplay pieces are found by attribute, not by path.** Every piece gameplay needs has a `Role` attribute plus `PlotIndex` or `ZoneIndex`: `Plot`, `PlotFloor`, `PlantBed` (two per plot, `BedIndex` 1 and 2; more added by hand with `BedIndex` 3, 4, ... are used too), `TreadmillBelt`, `TreadmillSign`, `PlotSign`, `UpgradeBoard`, and per zone `Nest` and `NestCore`. The plot origins, spawn points, seed pods, nest centres and guard homes are tagged Attachments (`Origin` (Role `PlotOrigin`: where plants are measured from) and `Spawn` on the plot floor, `Center`, `Pod1..Pod4` and `GuardHome` on the NestCore). `WorldBuilder.adopt()` rebuilds `PlotInfo`/`NestInfo` from them, so the pieces can be moved, rotated, reparented or re-modelled and gameplay follows. For example, the belt check uses the belt's current CFrame and size.
- **Validation and fallback.** Every plot `1..MAX_PLAYERS` and every zone must have all its roles. If one is missing, the server warns which plot or zone and which role, and regenerates the map from code for that session (`WorldBuilder.build()`), so the game never breaks. Duplicated roles are warned about, and the first one wins.
- **Zone boundaries come from `Config`**, not from the map: `zoneAtX`, the guards' chase lines and the corridor's ends don't change if a zone's floor or walls are moved. Only the look does.
- **Regenerating from code is explicit.** `lune run tools/build_map.luau` refuses to overwrite an existing `map/World.rbxm`. `--force` regenerates it from WorldBuilder/ZoneThemes/Props and **discards every hand edit in it** (git keeps the old file). The map tests are contract tests: the committed file loads, validates and gives usable plot and nest info (slot counts, belt check, pods). They don't compare it with the code, because hand edits are expected, with one exception: every Speed plank's text must match `Format.short(zone.req)`, so changing a zone's recommended Speed means regenerating the map (the client also rewrites the plank text at runtime).
- Guardians and plant-creatures are spawned at runtime, so they only appear in Play.

### UI icons (`icons/`)
The HUD and the world signs use the 21 PNG icons in `icons/` (256x256, "Free Icon Pack" by @gvesster). Roblox can only show **uploaded** images (`rbxassetid://<id>`), so each icon has an asset id in **`icons/ids.json`**. Rojo turns that file into the ModuleScript `ReplicatedStorage.Shared.IconIds` (Rojo 7 makes any plain `.json` file a ModuleScript returning its data; mapped in `default.project.json`), and `Config.ICONS` reads it. Updating ids never means editing Lua.

- **`0` = not uploaded yet: the emoji stays.** Every icon stands in for an emoji (or `R$`) in some text (`Icons.TOKENS`). While its id is 0 the text is shown exactly as before, so with every id at 0 (the repo default) the game looks the same as without icons.
- **One helper.** `Icons.image(key)` gives `"rbxassetid://<id>"` or nil. `Icons.set(label, keys, text, size?, textSize?)` writes every icon label: with no uploaded icon it is just `label.Text = text`; with one it empties the label and lays out an `IconRow` inside it: square `ImageLabel`s (`ScaleType.Fit`, `size` x the label height) and the text pieces, styled like the label (font, colour, stroke). The emoji can be in front (`🛒 Shop`), inline (`Buy · R$ 2,000` → Buy · [R$] 2,000), at both ends (`⚡ FASTEST HERE ⚡`), or above the text (`🏏\nBat`). One key whose emoji isn't in the text puts the icon in front (`$1.2K` → [Money] $1.2K, `-- SEED PACKS --`); a list of keys only replaces emoji that are there (so `{ "Lock" }` on "✅ Rec. Speed" adds nothing).
- **Sizes per context** (`ICON` in the HUD, x the label height): shop card titles big (1), HUD buttons, panels and section titles medium (0.85), R$ price buttons and inline lines small (0.7).
- **Where:** Shop/Index buttons, stalls and modal titles; the 🏃 stall and Trail Shop title; the Speed and money icons (bottom left); Speed/Money/Treadmill/pass cards; PLOT UPGRADE (card, gate board); every R$ button (Robux icon before the number); 🎁 gift buttons; 🌱/🪴 tabs and side titles; 🔒 on locked zones; the ✖ close button; the ✨ Index count; the 🏠 plot and 🏃 treadmill signs. (Emoji inside sentences are gone from the UI: see "UI text is words, not emoji".)
- **World signs** are set at runtime, so `map/World.rbxm` is untouched and keeps the emoji: `WorldBuilder.applyIcons` (end of `WorldBuilder.load`) gives the icon to the sign labels whose text is exactly one of `WorldBuilder.SIGN_ICONS` (the 3 stalls, each plot's plot sign and treadmill sign; hand-edited texts are left alone), and `PlotService` writes the plot and treadmill signs and the Upgrade boards (PlotUpgrade icon on the red button, Robux icon on the purple one, `WorldBuilder.setUpgradeBoard`) through `Icons.set`.

**Uploading** (Portuguese step by step: [icons/COMO_SUBIR_ICONES.md](icons/COMO_SUBIR_ICONES.md)):

```powershell
$env:ROBLOX_API_KEY = "<Open Cloud key with Assets read + write>"   # this terminal only, never in a file
$env:ROBLOX_USER_ID = "<your user id>"                              # or $env:ROBLOX_GROUP_ID for a group game
lune run tools/upload_icons.luau --dry-run   # what would be sent (no key, no network)
lune run tools/upload_icons.luau             # upload every icon whose id is still 0, write icons/ids.json
```
`tools/upload_icons.luau` (logic in `tools/icon_uploader.luau`) POSTs each PNG to the Open Cloud Assets API (`https://apis.roblox.com/assets/v1/assets`, multipart `request` JSON + `fileContent`, header `x-api-key`), polls `assets/v1/operations/<id>` until it is done and writes the id to `icons/ids.json` after each icon (a re-run skips icons that already have an id; `--force` and `--only Shop,Index` re-upload). It also keeps `icons/uploads.json` (asset ids and types, no secrets).

- **Image, not Decal.** The [Assets API docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/cloud/guides/usage-assets) list *Decal* and *Image* as separate upload types (same formats). An Image asset's id is what `ImageLabel.Image` takes, so the uploader uses `"assetType": "Image"`. A **Decal** id is a different asset that wraps the image: put in an ImageLabel it shows nothing in game (Studio's property box silently converts it when you paste, scripts don't). With `--type Decal` (or if Roblox answers with a decal) the uploader resolves the real image id: Open Cloud asset delivery (`GET apis.roblox.com/asset-delivery-api/v1/assetId/<decalId>` → `location`), downloads the decal and reads its `Texture` id. If that fails (e.g. the key lacks permission for asset delivery), ids.json keeps 0, the decal id stays in `icons/uploads.json`, and the guide explains how to get the image id in Studio.
- **The key** comes only from `ROBLOX_API_KEY`. It is never printed or written, and only sent to `apis.roblox.com`.
- **Manual way:** Studio → View → Asset Manager → Bulk Import the PNGs, right-click each → Copy Asset ID, paste into `icons/ids.json` under its file name.
- Uploaded images go through moderation; one that is still pending or rejected shows blank until approved.

### Drop-in models (`assets/`)
`assets/` (Rojo → `ServerStorage.Assets`) holds models made in Blender and saved from Studio (`<Slot>.rbxm`: a Model, or a Folder of variant Models). **For the model maker: [assets/COMO_ADICIONAR_MODELOS.md](assets/COMO_ADICIONAR_MODELOS.md)** (Portuguese, step by step, with the full slot table).

- Every repeated or distinctive map piece is built as a group Model tagged `AssetSlot` (its slot name) and `FitSize` (the space it uses). The groups are trees, bushes, rocks, flowers, lamps, fence posts and sections, each zone's props, the treadmill frame, the upgrade-sign stand and the shop stalls. `Assets.replace` swaps a group for the slot's model at the group's **current pivot**, so hand moves are kept. The model is scaled uniformly (`Model:ScaleTo`) to fit `FitSize` unless it has `KeepScale = true`, its bottom centre is the pivot, and `Yaw` (degrees) turns it. It is anchored, non-colliding for decor (fences and stalls keep their saved collision), stripped of scripts, and gets the built-in piece's lights and particles if it has none. Variants are picked from a stable hash of the position. File names match slots case- and accent-insensitively, with Portuguese aliases (`Arvores` → `Tree`, `Arbustos` → `Bush`, ...; `Assets.ALIASES`).
- **When they apply:** the server replaces every group still built from code at start (`WorldBuilder.load` → `Assets.refresh`). `lune run tools/apply_assets.luau` does the same in `map/World.rbxm`, keeping hand edits, so they show in edit mode; `--all` also re-places groups that already hold an older export. `build_map --force` uses them too.
- **Runtime-spawned models:** `Creature_<Seed>` (e.g. `Creature_LotusWyrm`) replaces a plant-creature, ahead of `Config.CREATURE_ASSETS`, and still gets rarity/mutation flair. `Guard_<Name>` (e.g. `Guard_GiantTurtle`) becomes a guardian's body, welded to the invisible root that `Humanoid:MoveTo` drives. The seven slots hold the owner's rigged pets (see "The zone bosses" below); a model without a `BossRig` attribute is fitted like any other and isn't animated.
- A broken model (no parts, empty folder, an error while fitting) is skipped with one `[Assets]` warning, and the built-in piece stays. Unknown file names are warned about, and `tests/assets.spec.luau` fails on them.
- Owner workflow when new models arrive: `lune run tools/apply_assets.luau`, then `rojo build -o StealASeed.rbxlx`, `lune run tests/run.luau`, commit `assets/` and `map/World.rbxm`. Meshes uploaded by the 3D Importer belong to the uploader's account: the experience's owner needs permission to use them in the published game.

## Rules the code enforces
- **Server-authoritative.** Money, Speed, pickup, carry, planting and purchases all live on the server. The client only draws the HUD and VFX and runs Slow Mode. Every prompt and remote re-checks distance, ownership and state, and remotes are rate-limited.
- **Speed stat vs WalkSpeed.** `WalkSpeed = clamp(16 + 27*log10(1 + Speed/100), 16, 180)`. Zones compare the *stat*.
- **Speed from moving** (`SpeedService` + `SpeedLogic`). Every 0.25 s the server measures horizontal HumanoidRootPart movement and grants `distance × Config.speedPerStud(trail)` (the flat `BASE_SPEED_PER_STUD` × the equipped trail, see Progression), capped per tick.
- **Movement sanity check.** A tick that moves more than `WalkSpeed × dt × 1.5 + 2` earns nothing. Sustained over-speed that drains a one-second movement budget, or any teleport-sized jump, is rubber-banded to the last valid position. Server-side moves (spawn, knockback) reset the tracker. A NaN or off-the-map position is rubber-banded too and never enters the tracker. The client moves its own character and can ignore a rubber-band, so every position check made between ticks (stealing a seed, getting home with it, selling, planting, the Upgrade boards, the bat) uses `SpeedService.trustedRoot`: the root only if the next tick wouldn't rubber-band it (`SpeedLogic.plausible`), and the guards chase where the server last saw the thief get to (`SpeedService.seenPosition`), so a teleport home shakes nothing off. A thief out past the corridor's walls (flying or clipping, where no guard can follow: `Inventory.offCourse`) loses the seed back to its pod. A character whose Humanoid disappears is respawned, and `Workspace.RejectCharacterDeletions` is on, so a client can't delete parts of its own character.
- **Treadmill.** The server checks whether the root part is inside **your own plot's** belt bounds and grants the treadmill level's fixed step × the trail every tick, in full (`SpeedLogic.treadmillGain`: +100 a step at level 1, up to +300 at level 10, 4 ticks a second; not WalkSpeed, not Speed, not how long the tick took). The belt does not move you: you just stand on it (AFK works) and the client loops your run animation in place, only on your own belt (`Shared/Treadmill.shouldRun`; the client knows its plot from StatsUpdate `plot` and the plot model's OwnerUserId). Someone else's belt pays nothing. There is no client remote.
- **Guards** (`GuardService` + `GuardBrain`). A guard wakes `Config.wakeDelay(k)` after a seed is taken (`WAKE_REACTION` = 0.5 s for the thief to notice and start running, then a 28-stud head start at the recommended Speed: 0.66–0.85 s, the Meadow's 2.25 s, showing ❗ meanwhile) and chases at `walkSpeedFor(zone.req) × GUARD_SPEED_FACTOR` (1.0), exactly a player's WalkSpeed at the recommended Speed. A server loop calls `Humanoid:MoveTo` along the corridor at 10 Hz, with no pathfinding. A catch is horizontal distance < 6 studs from the path the guard walked since its last tick, never `Touched`. A Spirit Garden guard covers 17 studs per tick, so checking only its current position could step right over a thief. The seed goes back to its pod, and the player is knocked 30 studs away and frozen for 1 s. The chase ends when the thief reaches the base, after a catch, or after 45 s, and then the guard walks home and sleeps.
- **Carry.** One seed at a time, a server-made part welded to your back. Dying or leaving returns it to its pod.
- **Rarities** (`Config.rarityWeights`). A pod rolls its rarity when it regrows. Common..Epic use `spawn × tilt^(i-1)` with `tilt = luck × (1 + 0.08·(zone-1))`, so deeper zones and night (`NIGHT_LUCK` = 2) give more Rares and Epics. **Legendary is a trophy**: a fixed chance per zone (`LEGENDARY_CHANCE`, × luck at night) that gets *rarer* down the corridor: 1% → 0.6% by day in zones 1–5, then **1 in 2,000** in Deep Space and **1 in 2,500** in the Spirit Garden (at most 0.1% at night). Epic pays ×10 and Legendary ×15 (Epic went from ×6 to ×10 so late-game income stays close to what it was with common Legendaries: the simulated free player ends about 6% lower, and reaches every zone within a few minutes of before). Planting a Legendary stolen in zones 6–7, or rolling one from a pack, announces it to the whole server (`Config.announcesLegendary`, `Net.announce`: "🌟 Alex got a LEGENDARY Nebula Rose!", or "...a LEGENDARY Seed!" from a pack, whose flower is still a surprise). `lune run tests/rarity_table.luau` prints the full odds table.
- **Growth.** Only `plantedAt` is saved. `weight = sprout × variance × (1 + 4·ease(age/20 min))` grows to 5× and stops. `income = seedIncome(zone) × rarity × mutation × sqrt(weight/sprout)`. A single payout loop evaluates the formula every second (`PAYOUT_INTERVAL` = 1) and pays each plant one second of the $/s on its billboard, silently (no popups over plants, owner 2026-10-15); a seedling earns nothing (see Sprouting). The Plants panel view is pushed every 3rd pass and when a seedling pops. There is no per-plant tick.
- **Day and night plants.** Every zone is either ☀️ day (Meadow, Desert, Lava, Spirit) or 🌙 night (Jungle, Snow, Space), set by `Zone.active`. Its plants pay `ACTIVE_TIME_MULT` during their phase and `OFF_TIME_MULT` during the other one. **Both are 1× since 2026-10-16** (owner: every plant earns the same day and night), so no plant is penalised and no label shows a Day/Night note; the rest of this item describes the mechanism if `OFF_TIME_MULT` is lowered again. Previously 0.8× (`Config.timeMult`, from the shared `dayPhase` clock). The payout loop applies it; while a plant is penalised its billboard, Plants card and Sell card add "Night x0.8" / "Day x0.8" in a muted grey (`Config.OFF_TIME_COLOR`), and nothing extra at x1. `plantIncome`/`incomeOf` stay the plain base, and the 30 s removal refund and the SELL stall's price use the base too. Offline income uses the cycle average: 300 s day and 150 s night give ×14/15 for day plants and ×13/15 for night plants.
- **Economy** is formulas in `Config` (see "Economy" above). `seedIncome(k) = ZONE_CASH[k+1] / (ECONOMY_SLOTS × 300 s) × INCOME_SCALE` with `ECONOMY_SLOTS = 10` and `INCOME_SCALE = 1.25` (it makes up for the x1 / x0.8 day/night rates): a 10-plant plot of zone-k Commons earns 1.25× zone k+1's `ZONE_CASH` in about 5 minutes. `ZONE_CASH` = 0, 10K, 500K, 5M, 50M, 500M, 5B: the recommended Speeds from before v7, kept so the money scale didn't move when the Speed scale shrank. Money buys trails, plot levels and treadmill tiers (`Config.priceOf`); plot levels cost `500 × 3.1^(L−2)` and multiply income by `1 + 0.06 × (L−1)`.
- **Data** (`ProfileStore`, format v13; v13 keeps each plant's palette, v10 keeps each bag seed's gene, v9 adds the plant inventory, v8 each plant's flower, v7 moves older saves' Speed onto the flat-gain scale, v6 saves each plant's spot instead of a fixed slot, v5 maps the old 3 treadmill tiers onto the 10 new ones, v4 added `freeGift`, `giftShown` and `chestAt`, see "Old saves" and "Community rewards"). `UpdateAsync` with retries and exponential backoff, autosave every 90 s, `BindToClose`. If a load fails, that session never saves, so it can't overwrite real data with defaults. Session lock: a loaded profile is stored with its session's `lock`; a save writes only while the lock is still its own (another server took the profile over, this session already let it go, or a newer save already landed: nothing is written, the session stops saving, and a Robux receipt waiting on that save isn't confirmed here). Leaving or shutting down releases it; a server loading a profile still held waits for that last save (up to ~8 s, then takes it over: the old server died), or takes it at once when the lock is older than 3 autosaves. Saved fields: money, Speed, owned trails + the equipped one, treadmill tier, plot level, plants (id, zone, rarity, mutation, plantedAt, its spot x/z on the plot, its flower, variance and palette), the inventory (up to 50 plants: zone, rarity, mutation, flower, age, variance, palette), Index, items (none are sold right now; only ids in `Config.START_ITEMS` load), the seed bag, the last 100 Robux receipt ids and `lastSeen`. v1 saves load with an empty bag and no receipts; v1/v2 saves get their Speed Gain tier turned into a trail and their slots into a plot level (see "Old saves" above). Potions and Sleep Dust were removed: potion timers and both items in old saves are dropped on load. Offline income is capped at 1 h and pays `Config.OFFLINE_MULT` (0.2x) of what the plants would make in game; on join a big gold "Welcome back! You made +$X while away" plate shows it (PlotService.grantOffline sets the player attributes `OfflineEarned` / `OfflineSeconds`, the HUD reads them).
- **Art.** The map is generated from primitives by `WorldBuilder` (base, plots), `ZoneThemes` (the 7 zones) and the `Props` kit, then hand-edited in `map/World.rbxm`, and any piece can be swapped for a model from `assets/` (see "Drop-in models"). Only floors and walls collide. All props are anchored and non-colliding (no Touched, no raycasts), so they never block runners, guards or the camera. The base is kept plain so the plants are the show (like a simulator base): a studded lawn in big checker tiles of two close greens, a sand walkway and plot paths, plain tan checker walls with a grass top, and chunky studded wooden fences (square plum-brown posts studded on every face, two thick rails) around each plot. Inside a plot: two pinkish-brown studded dirt beds with a light checker, left and right, and a lighter green grass path down the middle from the opening; no trees, bushes, flowers, lamps or rocks outside the corridor (tested). The base is compact: neighbouring plots touch and share one fence line (`PLOT_SPACING_X` = `PLOT_SIZE`; a plot builds its +X side only in the front column or beside the shop plaza, and a corner post is never built twice), the front plots end 24 studs before the corridor mouth (room for the free gift and the MOST MONEY/s board). The shop plaza takes the -Z side of the front column, by the corridor mouth (`Config.PLAZA_SPOT`), and the plots fill the other spots column by column, plot 1 nearest the mouth (`Config.plotSpot`), and each plot has one plain 16-stud opening toward the walkway with an orange-tan tile and the owner's sign on the fence beside it (no gate). The plots sit 24 studs from the walkway (`PLOT_OFFSET_Z` = 84): the treadmill stands in that strip, in front of its plot. Tests check that no two fence rails overlap, no two posts share a spot, no post pokes into a treadmill, board, sign or stall, every plot is fenced all round, and each treadmill (character-sized: belt about 4.5 × 9) stands in front of its plot, within its width, off the entrance path and clear of the walkway. The base area is about 1.5k parts (tested below 1.6k), the corridor about 3.6k and the whole map about 5.1k (tested below 6k/8k). The corridor's zones (`Zones/<Zone>`, on the shared `Zones/Kit`) each have 3-6 big set pieces and small details along the walls, every one built of many offset studded blocks at different heights (the same studded plastic as the floor and walls; owner playtest): the Meadow's lily pond, picket-fenced flower bed, mushrooms and round block trees; the Desert's stepped pyramid with its temple, ruins, palm oasis, giant skull, cacti, dunes and tumbleweeds; the Jungle's waterfall cliff, giant stone head and altar, giant trees with vines, bamboo and leaf plants; the Lava Fields' volcano altar (the owner's reference: lava zig-zagging down its steps, real fire on top), lava fall, lava terraces, vents, basalt columns, dead trees and ragged lava pools; the Snow Peaks' ice cliffs (one with a frozen waterfall), igloo, frozen pond, snowmen, pines and glowing crystals; Deep Space's UFO beaming up a cow, glowing craters, rocket, crystals, asteroids and blocky planets; the Spirit Garden's cherry trees, shrines, stone lanterns, koi pond, bamboo and waterfalls, and a pale back wall with a big waterfall and glowing lanterns so the end of the corridor shows from the spawn. `tests/zones.spec` checks every set piece stands clear of its nest, everything is studded, nothing z-fights (every face of the corridor, at any height) and each zone's parts, emitters and lights stay in budget. Lighting (`Shared/LightingLook`): two fixed looks, nothing moves across the sky: a clear, natural day that doesn't glare (Brightness 2, exposure −0.1, a plain ambient, faint bloom; the sun stands still at ClockTime 14), the default blue sky with clouds, crisp ShadowMap shadows, very thin air with no haze (Atmosphere density 0.05, so the whole corridor is visible from spawn; streaming is off) and lightly saturated colour (ColorCorrection saturation +0.12, contrast +0.04, no extra brightness), and the night is a clear, readable blue with the moon standing still at ClockTime 0 (bright blue ambient, stars, a little more bloom on the neon). ### Soft gates: how soft they are
A guard runs exactly as fast as a player at the zone's recommended Speed (`GUARD_SPEED_FACTOR = 1`). That player gets home on the head start alone. The seed pods lie right by the sleeping boss (a loose cluster a few studs off its right flank, owner's reference `docs/references/11-pods-by-the-boss.png`), so the head start is the guard's wake-up, its ❗: `Config.wakeDelay(k)` = `WAKE_REACTION` + `GUARD_HEAD_START / guardChaseSpeed(k)` + the zone's `wakeDelay` (0 in every zone now). `WAKE_REACTION` = **0.5 s** is the thief's time to notice: the Pickup reaching their client, a human reaction, and the server seeing their first steps (it sees a character about 0.1 s late). Until then they stand by the pod, 10–16 studs from the guard's root, and a late-zone guard covers 17 studs per tick. `GUARD_HEAD_START` = **28 studs**, the distance the pods used to lie ahead of the boss: a recommended-Speed thief runs 28 studs on top (0.35 s in the Desert, 0.16 s in the Spirit Garden, 1.75 s in the Meadow).

Why `WAKE_REACTION` (owner playtest, 100M in the Spirit Garden, caught "as soon as I take the seed"): the wake-up used to be only the head start plus 0–0.1 s, 0.26 s in the Spirit Garden. The old chase sim started the thief running at the very instant of the steal, from the pod, with the server seeing them live, so it showed 0.4 s of slack. In the real game the thief was still standing by the pod when the guard woke, and its first tick or two caught them, at any Speed. `tests/steal_flow.luau` now runs the steal the way the game does: any spot in the prompt's reach (even inside the sleeping boss: players pass through guards), a reaction delay, 0.1 s of server lag through the movement tracker (`SpeedService.seenPosition`), the guard loop at 10 Hz at any phase and its `MoveTo` walked every physics frame. The catch is still measured from the guard's root (the middle of its body, inside the model's width; the pods stay outside it).

A slower player keeps the same head start but loses ground every second, and **below the sign the guard runs faster after them** (`Config.guardSpeedVs`: (sign / Speed) ^ 0.6, at most x1.35; owner 2026-10-16, 5M outran the 7.5M Snow Peaks; the exponent went from 0.3 to 0.6 on 2026-10-19 after a 65M player farmed the 75M Spirit Garden). A guard walks home at 1.2x its chase speed (`GUARD_RETURN_FACTOR`, 2x the old 0.6). WalkSpeed is logarithmic in Speed, so a few WalkSpeed points are a big share of the Speed number. In the steal flow (slack: how late a recommended-Speed thief may start running, server lag on top; threshold: the lowest Speed a quick thief, 0.2 s, escapes with):

| Zone | Recommended | Wakes after (reaction + head start) | Escapes from Speed | WalkSpeed (player vs guard) | Slack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desert | 25K | 0.85 s (0.5 + 0.35) | ~20.9K (84%) | 78.7 vs 80.8 | 0.77 s |
| Jungle | 500K | 0.74 s (0.5 + 0.24) | ~454K (91%) | 114.7 vs 115.9 | 0.61 s |
| Lava | 2.5M | 0.71 s (0.5 + 0.21) | ~2.34M (93%) | 134.0 vs 134.7 | 0.67 s |
| Snow | 7.5M | 0.69 s (0.5 + 0.19) | ~7.1M (95%) | 147.0 vs 147.6 | 0.57 s |
| Deep Space | 25M | 0.67 s (0.5 + 0.17) | ~23.9M (96%) | 161.2 vs 161.7 | 0.64 s |
| Spirit Garden | 75M | 0.66 s (0.5 + 0.16) | ~72M (96%) | 174.2 vs 174.6 | 0.55 s |

Why the wake-up and not the other knobs: a slower guard (`GUARD_SPEED_FACTOR` < 1) would let a thief below the sign pull away on long runs and break "you need the recommended Speed", and a ramp-up acceleration is a head start too, just harder to read. The wake-up already exists (the ❗ the boss shows; it now lasts about as long as its 0.6 s wake-up hop). Slack trades against the threshold: every 0.1 s of it lowers the threshold, and the shorter the run home, the less a slower thief loses on the way. With the short zones (runs home of 264 studs from the Desert nest to 2,240 from the Spirit Garden's) a quick thief needs ~84–96% of the sign: a thief can't be far below it. The Meadow (recommended 0) wakes after 2.25 s, and its run home is a tutorial: anyone who runs gets away.

The tests check, in the steal flow (every pod, stance and loop phase):
- 100M and 75M in the Spirit Garden escape running 0.3 s after the steal (with the old 0.26 s wake-up they are caught in the guard's first ticks, at any Speed);
- at the recommended Speed and 4/3 of it, starting to run 0.45 s after the steal escapes in zones 2–7;
- the previous zone's recommended Speed (reacting at once), 50% of the sign (the Desert 40%; reacting in 0.2 s) and standing by the pod get caught in zones 2–7.

And in the ideal chase sim (the thief runs at the instant of the steal, from the pod): slack of at least 0.3 s, and 70% of the recommended WalkSpeed and the previous zone's recommended Speed get caught. It ticks at 10 Hz, like the guard loop, so delays act in 0.1 s steps. Keep `GUARD_SPEED_FACTOR` at 1 or below, or players at exactly the recommended Speed get caught.

## The zone bosses: the owner's rigged pets, animated by code
The seven guardians are rigged pets the owner bought (`models/bosses/Pets.rbxl`, Workspace.PetTemplates; no scripts): skinned MeshParts deformed by Bones. `lune run tools/export_bosses.luau` exports the chosen pet of each zone (`BossRigs.BY_ZONE`) into its `assets/Guard_<Name>.rbxm`: just the meshes and the RootPart with the Bones (the kit's AnimationController, saved animations, Motor6Ds and helper parts are dropped), scaled by the profile (part sizes and bone offsets), anchored, front = -Z, with `BossRig`, `RigScale`, `KeepScale` and `Pivot = (0,0,0)` (Assets.place puts the pet's own ground point, not its bounds' centre, at the guard's feet).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🌼 Meadow | Giant Turtle | Turtle | 10 × 14 × 22 | trot; asleep its head pulls into the shell, legs tucked |
| 🦂 Desert | Red Mole | RedMole | 11 × 28 × 34 | splayed digging paws paddle; whiskers twitch; rears up to bite |
| 🦖 Jungle | Giant Snake | GiantSnake | 14 × 8 × 33 | slithers (a wave down the body and tail, the head held steady); coils its neck down asleep; strikes |
| 🌋 Lava Fields | Volcano Gorilla | GorillaVulcano | 15 × ~27 × 12 | arms brought down from the kit's T-pose; knuckle gallop; chest beat on waking; two-fisted smash |
| ❄️ Snow Peaks | Ice Shark | IceShark | 15 × 24 × 32 | glides over the snow: tail sweeps, fins paddle, body bobs; jaws snap |
| 👽 Deep Space | Void Dragon | VoidDragon | 18 × 29 × 17 | runs on two legs flapping its wings; folds them and crouches asleep; wings flare on a bite |
| ⛩️ Spirit Garden | Lumistag | Lumistag | 18 × 14 × 13 | gallops in bounds; lies down with folded legs; antler ram |

The server still moves the invisible HumanoidRootPart with `Humanoid:MoveTo` (gameplay unchanged) with the pet welded to it, and tags the guard with `Rig`, `BodyMin`/`BodyMax` and, on a catch, `CatchAt`. Every client poses the Bones locally (`src/client/GuardAnimator.client.luau`, only bosses within 150 studs of the camera) from the guard's `State` attribute: sleeping (lying down, slow breathing, floating Zzz), waking (a hop, head flung up, mouth open; the ❗ label stays), chasing (the gait, its stride phase driven by ground speed, with dust and footstep thumps), the catch lunge/bite, a grumpy slower walk home, and a dizzy wobble when stunned, cross-faded over 0.25 s. `src/shared/BossRigs.luau` holds a rig profile per pet (which bones are the legs L/R front/back, spine, neck, head, jaw, tail, wings, fins) and is pure math: a pose turns bones about their own pivots in model axes, and `solve` turns it into `Bone.Transform`. The kits' feet and hands are IK-target bones parented to the root, so they "follow" the end of their leg, feet stay flat, and no foot, toe, hand or finger goes under the ground. Near a side wall the body slides inward a little so it never pokes through (visual only). `tests/bosses.spec.luau` checks each slot holds its pet with no scripts or animation leftovers, every profile bone exists, the sizes, and that every state's pose is finite and stays above the ground.

## Plant-creatures
Each planted seed grows into its zone's creature (`CreatureModels`). The creatures are 20–32 parts (up to 39 with Legendary flair) built from Parts, WedgeParts and built-in Sphere `SpecialMesh`es, so nothing needs uploading. Each one hatches from a seed husk and has a leaf or flower motif. Later zones stand taller and glow more.

| Zone | Creature |
|---|---|
| Meadow | **Sproutling**: round green bulb with a two-leaf sprout and yellow bud, leaf arms, rosy cheeks |
| Desert | **Cactopod**: barrel cactus on four stubby legs, saguaro arms, spines, pink flower crown |
| Jungle | **Fernosaur**: long-necked leaf dino, fern fronds down its back, fiddlehead tail, drifting spores |
| Lava Fields | **Magmabloom**: magma-rock body with Neon lava seams and fists, sunflower head of Neon flame petals, embers |
| Snow Peaks | **Frostbulb**: icy teardrop bulb in a red scarf, Neon ice-crystal crown, falling snow |
| Deep Space | **Starpetal**: floating cyclops star-flower on a Neon stem, five star petals, glowing antennae, two moons |
| Spirit Garden | **Lotus Wyrm**: coral dragon coiling out of a lotus on a lily pad, gold horns/whiskers/fins, spirit pearl, petals |

- **Rarity**: Uncommon recolours the trims in the rarity colour (no aura ring: nothing flat lies on the ground under a plant). Rare makes the trims Neon and adds a light. Epic adds sparkles. Legendary adds a halo of orbs.
- **Mutation chances** (owner 2026-10-19: first 2.5x "for more variety", then "half of what they are right now", `Config.MUTATIONS`, rolled in this order; packs double the pod ones, but not Celestial): **Gold** 5% of pods (×3 income), **Diamond** 2% (×4), **Rainbow** 1.25% (×8), Night 12% of night pods (×2), **Celestial** 0.625% of pods and of pack seeds (×12), Haunted only from the Halloween card (×5). The rarer, the better it pays; the free player's timeline stays where it was (`tests/progression`, the Spirit Garden at ~12.7 h). `tests/mutations.spec` checks the chances, Diamond in every place a mutation shows (names, odds, the Index, Sell, the bud, saves) and the makeovers.
- **Gold and Diamond makeovers** (owner 2026-10-19: "golden types diamond types besides the mutant halloween types"; `Shared/Mutations`, used by `CreatureModels` and `FlowerView` like `Halloween.hauntify`, so the planted plant and its 3D pictures match): a whole new look for the plant model, by colour (whatever the parts' order) and never on our Flair (halo, jack-o'-lantern). Each colour goes onto a ramp by its brightness, half absolute and half against the plant's own range, lifted so most of the plant is the middle of the ramp (over the whole garden: ~9% dark, ~60% middle, ~30% bright). **Gold** (`goldify`): darks deep amber (Metal), the middle rich gold and brights pale or white gold (Foil), Neon bright gold glow, its particles golden sparkles. **Diamond** (`diamondify`): darks deep ice blue and the middle icy cyan (Ice), brights white crystal (Glass, 25% see-through at most: never invisible), Neon cyan-white glow, its particles white-cyan glints. A built-in creature (no plant models) is still tinted by its parts' roles. `lune run tools/haunted_gallery.luau <out.rbxl> Gold` (or `Diamond`, or no name: Haunted) builds a place with every plant in that look.
- **Mutations**: Gold turns the creature to gold (see above). Diamond turns it to icy crystal. Rainbow cycles its skin on the client and gives the leaves rainbow hues. Night gives it a deep-blue tint. Celestial (the rarest: packs and pods) is the rarest look: a deep-violet body with a starry sheen, white Neon leaves and petals, a halo of white star orbs and slow drifting stars. Every mutation adds sparkles and a light, and the model keeps its `Mutation` attribute.
- **Colour variants** (`Shared/Palettes`, owner 2026-10-19: more visual variety, late-game gardens looked copy-pasted and the flower pack is fully used): a seed's gene picks a palette as well as its flower: **Natural** (the model as it is, 44% of seeds) or **Crimson, Amber, Azure, Violet** (14% each; Amber, not Gold, because Gold is a mutation). The pick hashes the gene with an integer hash (`Palettes.of`, the same on server and client), independent of the flower pick (`(gene-1) % n + 1`) and the weight variance, so every flower comes in every palette (tested over all genes). The plant keeps it (`palette`, save v13; `PlantGhosts.seedPlant`, the inventory keeps it too); plants from before have none and stay Natural forever, and so does a seed from before genes. The recolour (`Palettes.apply`) is by colour, whatever the parts' order, like `Halloween.hauntify`: it turns the plant's biggest colour family onto the palette's hue and every other coloured part by the same angle (its own parts, particles, beams and lights; never our halo or jack-o'-lantern), keeping every colour's brightness and saturation, so Neon stays as bright and the plant keeps its contrasts. A flower with green leaves turns its blossom only (when the blossom is at least 15% of the surface), so the leaves stay green; an all-green plant turns entirely; a near-colourless one (under 10% coloured, the Crystal Daisy) gets a pale wash of the palette on its light whites. A plant already of the palette's hue still turns 25°, away from the leaves' greens. Haunted, Gold, Diamond and Rainbow repaint the whole plant, so a seed of any of them has no palette (and an older Gold plant's palette no longer shows: it says "Gold Daisy"); Night and Celestial tint over it (the palette still shows through, tested). The name says it before the flower and after the mutation ("Azure Duality Rose", "Night Azure Duality Rose"; Natural says nothing), and so do the Plants, Inventory and Sell cards' 3D pictures (`FlowerView.flower(..., palette)`, a recoloured copy of the preview made once). The Index records each palette grown (`zone:rarity:Daisy:Azure`) and shows a stud per palette in the flower's cell, lit in its colour once found; the discovered count stays one per flower. Purely cosmetic: income, weight and price never read it. `tests/palettes.spec` checks the distribution, the independence, server = ghost = picture, old saves, the mutations and, for every model in every palette, the contrast (brightness and saturation kept, one turn for all, the blossom stays apart from the leaves, at least 10% of the plant turns, a quarter of that on the palette's hue). `lune run tools/build_thumbnails.luau <folder> SpiritEpics` builds `PaletteLineup_SpiritEpics.rbxl`, the Spirit Garden's three Epics in every palette side by side, ready to screenshot in Studio.

- **Size**: `CreatureModels.scaleFor` gives about 1× at sprout and about 1.9× full-grown, +7% per rarity step, clamped to `CREATURE_MAX_SCALE`, then times its rarity's `CREATURE_RARITY_SIZE` (`Config.creatureSizeMult`: Common..Epic 1×, **Legendary 1.5×** at every stage, owner 2026-10-15). A full-grown Legendary (the zone's tallest model) is ~35-41 studs (Meadow 38, Desert 37.5, Jungle 41, Lava 35, Snow 37, Space 36.5) and ~45 in the Spirit Garden, ~18-24 when it sprouts; its planting ghost uses the same scale. It gets room to grow: planting keeps plants apart by their full-grown size (`Planting.radius`, tested against `scaleFor`).
- **Idle sway**: `Effects.client` runs one loop that bobs and sways every creature within 160 studs of the camera, using one `BulkMoveTo`. It runs on the client only, so nothing replicates.
- **Uploaded models**: a `Creature_<Seed>.rbxm` in `assets/` comes first (see "Drop-in models"). Otherwise, asset ids in `Config.CREATURE_ASSETS` (zone index → id) are loaded once. Both are fitted to the footprint and decorated like the procedural creatures, and the procedural creature is the fallback.
- `lune run tests/creature_view.luau [zone] [rarity] [mutation]` prints ASCII front and side views, so you can check silhouettes without Studio. `tests/creatures.spec.luau` checks part budgets, footprint, heights, flair and scaling.

## Networking
`ReplicatedStorage.Remotes`:
- Client → server: `RequestBuy(itemId)`, `RequestUseItem(itemId)`.
  - `RequestBuy` ids: `PlotUpgrade` and `Trail:<id>` (money), `Product:<name>` (developer products, Robux only), `Pass:<name>` (gamepasses). For Robux the server only opens the prompt; `ProcessReceipt` grants.
  - `RequestUseItem` ids: `Bat`, `Seed:<bagKey>` (plant from the seed bag), `Equip:<trailId>` (an owned trail).
- Server → client: `StatsUpdate(partialStats)` (the client merges it), `LootEvent(kind, data)`, `IncomePopup(amount, position)`. Pack openings are `LootEvent("PackRoll", { packId, name, zone, result })`.
- Client → server: `SellPlants(slots, ids?, seeds?)`, the Sell window's plant ids, inventory ids and seeds by label (see "Selling plants"). `RequestBuy("GrowNow:<plant id>")`: the Grow now prompt for one of your seedlings. Server → client: `LootEvent("Sprouted", { slot, name, pos })` when a seedling pops.
- Client → server: `RemovePlant(slot)`, the Plants panel's Remove button, from anywhere: rate-limited (4/s), and `Inventory.take` checks the slot is a whole number naming one of the player's own plants; the plant goes to the **inventory** as it is (flower, mutation, growth), never for money. `StatsUpdate` carries `catalog` (the Index's flowers per zone and rarity) once at join, each plant in `plants` has its `name`, and `inventory` lists the inventory plants (`id`, `name`, `sell`, ...).
- Client → server: `PlantSeed(position, bagKey?)`, from the planting preview: plant the carried seed (or bagged seed `bagKey`, or inventory plant `"inv:<id>"`) at `position` (rate-limited; `PlotService.plantAt` re-checks everything). Server → client: `LootEvent("PlaceSeed", { key, zone, rarity, name, scale })` after the Inventory's Plant button, to show the ghost preview for that seed or plant. `SellPlants(slots, ids?)` sells planted plants and inventory plants at the SELL stall.
- Client → server: `AdminEvent(request)` (the admin panel's EVENTS tab: `start` / `stop` / `announce`, admins only; see "Live events"). Server → client: `LootEvent("LiveAnnounce", { text, by })` to everyone, and `ReplicatedStorage.LiveEvents` (the running events).
- Client → server: `ClaimReward("Gift")`, the Free Gift's CLAIM! button (rate-limited; the server checks the community). Server → client: `LootEvent("OpenFreeGift")` (the once-per-account auto-open), `LootEvent("FreeGift", { result, claimed })`; `StatsUpdate` carries `freeGift`.
- Stealing uses server-side ProximityPrompts (removing a plant is the Plants panel's button, above); the Upgrade boards use ClickDetectors and popup-less prompts. Day/night is computed from the shared clock (`Config.dayPhase`), so it needs no remote.

## Known limits
- **Not playtested in Studio yet.** The instance code (world, guard humanoids, prompts, UI) compiles and type-checks against the Roblox API definitions. Only the pure logic is covered by tests.
- After a server crash, the player's next server waits about 8 s on the old session lock before taking the profile over (whatever the crashed server hadn't saved, up to one autosave, is lost).
- The guards are welded primitives that glide, with no walk animation. The built-in art is primitives and built-in `rbxasset://` particle textures; drop-in models from `assets/` replace it piece by piece.
- **Drop-in models are checked under Lune only.** Lune has no `Model:ScaleTo`/`PivotTo`, so the baked map places models by moving and resizing their parts, while the server uses `ScaleTo`/`PivotTo`. Both put the bottom centre on the pivot and fit the same box, but the Roblox path has not been run in Studio yet.
